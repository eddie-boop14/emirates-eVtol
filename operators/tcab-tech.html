TCab Tech (Shanghai Time Technology Co.)

TCab Tech is a Shanghai-based eVTOL developer building the E20, a five-seat tilt-rotor aircraft. Its only documented United Arab Emirates position is a memorandum of understanding with Autocraft, the Abu Dhabi aerial-mobility brand of the technology group K2, covering up to 350 aircraft for the UAE and wider MENA market. No TCab aircraft has been shown by a primary source to have flown in the Emirates, and the E20's certification work sits with the Civil Aviation Administration of China, not the GCAA.

About

TCab Tech is a Chinese eVTOL developer, headquartered in Shanghai, whose product is the E20 — an all-electric tilt-rotor carrying one pilot and four passengers. The company's own material gives a maximum speed of 320 km/h, a maximum range of 200 km and a 450 kg payload, with an 800-volt charging architecture. Its two most significant flight-test milestones are its own: a first crewed flight test announced on 17 October 2025, flown by the company's chief test pilot with founder and chief executive Yon Wui Ng aboard, and completion of transition flight testing on a full-scale demonstrator announced on 3 December 2025. Neither announcement states where the flights took place, and neither mentions the United Arab Emirates.

The UAE connection runs through Autocraft. TCab announced a memorandum of understanding with Autocraft on 6 November 2024 to explore commercial applications of the E20 in the Emirates, and a second, larger memorandum on 16 July 2025 covering up to 350 E20 aircraft for the UAE and the wider MENA region. TCab's own release is explicit that this is a memorandum of understanding rather than a firm purchase order, gives no delivery schedule beyond 'the coming years', and discloses no contract value. Widely circulated figures describing this as a 'one billion dollar order' for 350 aircraft appear only in trade press; no primary source published by either party states a value. This reference site therefore records a memorandum for up to 350 aircraft and no monetary figure.

The identity of the counterparty is worth stating precisely, because the two sides describe it differently. TCab's July 2025 release calls Autocraft a 'state-owned UAE technology enterprise'. The Dubai Airshow organiser's own advanced air mobility preview of 28 August 2025 instead quotes Waleed Alblooshi of K2 describing Autocraft as 'a UAE subsidiary brand under K2 based in Abu Dhabi', and K2's corporate website presents Autocraft as its aviation vertical. K2 is a privately presented Abu Dhabi technology group; no primary document located establishes state ownership. The 'state-owned' characterisation is not published here.

On the Dubai Airshow 2025 question, the primary record supports an Autocraft-badged E20 exhibit rather than a TCab-badged one. The airshow organiser's preview carries K2's statement that it would 'showcase our eVTOL innovation through Autocraft' and unveil an eVTOL model alongside a lounge-style vertiport experience at its stand. TCab's own release of 6 November 2025 states that 'the customized E20 eVTOL has successfully completed its testing phase and is now ready for deployment, marking a major milestone ahead of its upcoming global debut at the Dubai Airshow.' Neither statement describes a flying display, and neither describes flight in UAE airspace. Trade-press reporting that the aircraft was 'nearing first flight in the UAE' is not corroborated by any primary source and is not published here. K2 separately announced on 27 November 2025 a strategic partnership with Abu Dhabi Aviation Group and Multi Level Group to accelerate the Autocraft E20+.

Regulatory reality is the clearest part of the picture. The E20's airworthiness work is Chinese: TCab announced that a type certification team meeting chaired by CAAC experts was held on 10 December 2024 and that an Aircraft Evaluation Group review covering operational compliance, pilot training and maintenance standards had been launched. TCab has not published a target certification year. Nothing in any GCAA, TCab, Autocraft or K2 primary document located indicates that the GCAA has accepted an application, issued a validation, granted a test licence or approved any operation involving the E20. Until such a document exists, TCab Tech's UAE status is an announced commercial intention, not an aviation programme.

Frequently asked questions

Is the TCab Tech and Autocraft partnership real, or a misremembering?
It is real and documented on both sides. TCab Tech published a memorandum of understanding with Autocraft on 6 November 2024 and a second covering up to 350 E20 aircraft on 16 July 2025, and K2 presents Autocraft as its Abu Dhabi aviation brand. What is not established is the widely repeated one-billion-dollar valuation.
Did TCab Tech order 350 aircraft worth USD 1 billion from the UAE?
No primary source says so. TCab's own announcement describes a memorandum of understanding for up to 350 E20 aircraft with no value disclosed and no delivery schedule. The billion-dollar figure and the framing as a firm order come from trade press only and are treated here as unverified.
Was the E20 at Dubai Airshow 2025?
An Autocraft-badged eVTOL was exhibited. The Dubai Airshow organiser's own preview quotes K2 saying it would showcase its eVTOL through Autocraft and unveil a model at its stand, and TCab's own release refers to the customised E20's 'global debut at the Dubai Airshow'. No primary source describes the aircraft flying at the show or in UAE airspace.
Has the E20 flown in the UAE?
Not according to any primary source located. TCab's crewed flight test of 17 October 2025 and its transition flight tests of 3 December 2025 are announced without a stated location, and neither release mentions the Emirates. Trade-press claims that a UAE first flight is imminent are not published here.
Is the E20 certified, and by whom?
It is not certified. Certification is being pursued with the Civil Aviation Administration of China; TCab announced a CAAC type certification team meeting on 10 December 2024 and the launch of an Aircraft Evaluation Group review. No completion date has been published by the company.
Does the GCAA have any role in the E20 programme?
None that is documented. No GCAA type certificate, validation, design acceptance, test licence or operational approval relating to TCab Tech, the E20 or Autocraft appears in any primary source located. Any UAE service would require GCAA action that is not yet on the public record.

Verification flags

  • The 'USD 1 billion' / 'firm order for 350 eVTOLs' framing appears only in trade press. TCab's own release says memorandum of understanding, 'up to 350', value undisclosed. Not published.
  • TCab's July 2025 release calls Autocraft a 'state-owned UAE technology enterprise'; the Dubai Airshow organiser and K2's own site describe Autocraft as a brand of the Abu Dhabi group K2. State ownership is unconfirmed and not published.
  • No primary source states where TCab's crewed flight test (17 October 2025) or transition flight tests (3 December 2025) took place. A UAE location is not established.
  • Trade-press reports that the Autocraft-backed E20 was 'nearing first flight in the UAE' are uncorroborated by any primary source and are not published.
  • The seed note placing TCab Tech at 'Dubai International Airport' has no primary basis. Dubai Airshow 2025 was held at the Dubai Airshow Site, DWC, and no vertiport association is documented.
  • Aircraft naming differs between parties: TCab publishes 'E20', Autocraft/K2 market an 'E20+'. Whether the E20+ is a distinct UAE variant or a marketing name is not established by any primary document.
  • No GCAA type certificate, validation, design acceptance, test licence or operational approval relating to TCab Tech or the E20 was located.
  • Autocraft operates no primary newsroom of its own that could be located; the UAE side of the partnership is documented only through K2's corporate site and the Dubai Airshow organiser.
  • TCab has published no target year for CAAC type certification, and no delivery date for any UAE aircraft.
  • K2's announced partnership with Abu Dhabi Aviation Group and Multi Level Group (27 November 2025) is listed on K2's own site; the underlying release page could not be retrieved directly, so its terms are not published.
