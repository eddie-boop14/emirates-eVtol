## The state of the race, in one paragraph

Dubai is one regulatory step from launch: the world's first certified
commercial vertiport (VDX at DXB, certified by the GCAA on 7 July 2026) is
ready, Joby's piloted test campaign is complete, booking will run through the
Uber app — and the operator still says first passengers fly **before the end
of 2026**. Abu Dhabi is close behind: the GCAA moved Archer's Midnight into a
Restricted Type Certificate programme on 7 May 2026 — a pathway that can allow
limited commercial operations before full type certification. Qatar is a
phase earlier: the EH216-S pilotless trials over central Doha (November 2025)
now sit inside a fresh statutory framework, Law No. 10 of 2026, which gives
the QCAA explicit licensing power over unmanned aircraft.

## What changed since our last full verification (May → August)

- **VDX certified, 7 July** — the GCAA issued the first purpose-built
  commercial vertiport certificate anywhere; capacity up to 170,000
  passengers/year. *(Skyports release; The National)*
- **American University of Dubai vertiport is under construction** — and we
  can now say, from the RTA's own wording, that it IS the "Dubai Marina" node:
  "developed at Dubai Marina, within the American University in Dubai parking
  area, managed by Wasl Asset Management Group." *(RTA)*
- **Archer's RTC programme entry (7 May)** — a programme entry, not a granted
  certificate, whatever some headlines said. Abu Dhabi Aviation remains the
  launch operator. *(Archer IR; Gulf News)*
- **Qatar's Law No. 10 of 2026 (19–20 July)** — statutory QCAA authority over
  unmanned-aircraft licensing, zones, altitudes and routes. It never names
  air taxis; it is the framework they would be licensed under. *(The
  Peninsula; Gulf Times)*

## The number everyone quotes that nobody has sourced

**"AED 350."** The circulating Dubai launch fare appears in no RTA, Joby or
Uber publication we could find — and it contradicts the RTA's own on-record
position (20 Nov 2025): *"We have not decided the price yet, but it will be
cheaper than helicopters today in Dubai,"* with a long-term aim of Uber/Careem
parity. Our sourced fare tracker: evtolemirates.com/explainers/dubai-air-taxi-price.html

## Watch list to year-end

1. **Joby's GCAA operational certification** — the last regulatory step the
   operator has named before Dubai passenger service.
2. **GCAA's Q3 2026 air-taxi certification target** (stated 3 Nov 2025) —
   completion not yet announced.
3. **AUH & AZI vertiports (Abu Dhabi)** — Q1 2026 completion targets passed
   without a completion announcement; BETA's first Charge Cube is already
   commissioned at Al Bateen.
4. **Dubai Airshow + VFS International AAM Conference, 24–26 Nov, Dubai** —
   the year's biggest regional attention window.
