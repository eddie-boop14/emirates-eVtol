   entity pages (5 locales) carried a hand-written "Last verified" date older

   has moved past it; same invariant checks it.

   Vertiport — linked to an anchor that did not exist on `dxb-vertiport` and
   `gulf-evtol-comparison-uae-vs-qatar` (all locales); `[joby-ir-2025-11-17]`
   likewise on `dubai-marina-vertiport`. Source entries added; guard
   invariant 32 now requires every in-page anchor to resolve.
4. **Sources listed on a page but absent from the dataset** (so absent from
   the API): `rta-statement-2025-11` on `margham-to-al-maktoum`,
   `skyports-2026-07-07` on `rta-dubai`. Added to `entities.json`.
5. **Doha trial date.** Three pages dated Qatar's EH216-S flights to 17 Nov
   2025 (EHang's release date); the MoT release dates the flight to 15 Nov.
   Corrected in `gulf-evtol-comparison-uae-vs-qatar`, `did-not-make-the-cut`
   and `ehang-eh216-s-mecca`, 5 locales, JSON-LD included. Joby's own 17 Nov
   2025 release is a different event and is untouched. See the Qatar repo's
   note of the same date for the sourcing.
